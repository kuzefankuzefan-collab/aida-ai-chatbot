About AIDA

Target Users

High school students, introductory college students, or curious beginners who want clear, jargon-free explanations of Artificial Intelligence terms.

Problem It Solves

Technical jargon in data science and AI can feel overwhelming. AIDA breaks down topics like Supervised Learning, Neural Networks, and Generative AI into plain English with real-world analogies (like flashcards, bike riding, and recipes).

How to Use

Click on any prompt button at the top or ask your own question in the input box below. Try typing keywords like "bias", "deep learning", or "AI vs ML"!
